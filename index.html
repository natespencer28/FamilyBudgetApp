How to use overtime

Until the initial emergency fund goal is met, consider splitting overtime between savings and the highest-interest debt.

Once the goal is reached, consider directing most overtime toward debt while reserving some for family needs.

Do not use a debt payoff estimate until you have confirmed the loan interest rates.
